The 2026-05-05 Zenodo preprint at DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20011107 is co-authored
by Arash Kermani Kolankeh and Rita Zgheib (both CUD). However the **Khayyam
Math software** (formerly SeVim / Sevim Studio, deployed at khayyammath.com)
that has been built since the preprint is **solely Arash's work** — Dr.
Zgheib did not participate in the implementation, source code, architecture,
training pipeline, or production deployment.

**Why:** User explicitly clarified during the UAE IP registration filing
that Rita has not participated in this work. The preprint described an
earlier conceptual framework; the registered software substantially evolved
from it but is independently authored.

**How to apply:** When drafting filings, citations, NOTICE files, paper
authorship, or anything else that asks "who wrote Khayyam Math":
- The software author is Arash alone.
- Cite the preprint as a related/predecessor work, not as joint authorship
  of the current software.
- Do not state that Rita assigned rights — she had nothing to assign.
- The preprint citation itself remains joint-authored (that's a published
  fact); only the software is sole-authored.
